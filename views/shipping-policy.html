01 Order Processing

  • Processing: 1–2 working days from payment confirmation.
  • Working days: Monday–Saturday, excluding Indian public holidays.
  • Cut-off: payments after 15:00 IST begin processing the next working day.
  • Special instructions may add 1–2 days; we email you if so.

03 Delivery Timelines

Total time equals processing plus transit. A typical Delhi NCR order arrives in 2–5 calendar days; rest-of-India orders in 4–9 calendar days.

04 Tracking

Your order moves through five visible stages — Order Placed → Confirmed → Shipped → Out for Delivery → Delivered — shown as a live timeline in My Orders. We email you at dispatch, out-for-delivery and delivery.

05 Packaging & Insurance

Every order travels in signature Bhuvi Boutique packaging: tissue-wrapped, dust-bagged where applicable, and sealed in a tamper-evident outer. All shipments are insured for their full invoice value at no extra cost.

06 Delivery Attempts

Our courier makes up to three attempts. If all fail, the parcel returns to us and we contact you to reship (you pay the delivery fee again) or refund the item value minus the outbound shipping cost.

07 Address Accuracy

You are responsible for the accuracy of the shipping address and phone number. We cannot redirect a parcel once it is out for delivery. If returned to us because of an incorrect address, we refund the item value less outbound shipping.

08 Delays & Force Majeure

Timelines are estimates, not guarantees. Events outside our control may delay delivery. If an order is delayed beyond 15 days, you may cancel for a full refund.

09 Lost or Damaged Parcels

If tracking shows delivered but you have not received the parcel, or it arrives damaged, tell us within 48 hours with photographs. We either reship free or refund in full.

10 International Shipping

We currently ship within India only. International delivery is on our roadmap; use the contact form and we will notify you when it launches.
