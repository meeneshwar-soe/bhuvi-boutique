A Terms & Conditions

This Part A governs your use of the Bhuvi Boutique website and your purchases from us. By creating an account or placing an order, you agree to these terms together with our Return & Refund Policy and Shipping Policy.

01 Agreement to Terms

Bhuvi Boutique ("we", "us") operates this website from New Delhi, India. By browsing, creating an account or placing an order you confirm you are at least 18 years old and legally able to enter into a binding contract.

02 Accounts & Security

  • You must provide accurate, current information at registration and keep it updated.
  • You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for all activity under your account.
  • One account per person. We may suspend accounts used fraudulently.
  • Notify us immediately at meeneshwar2007@gmail.com if you suspect unauthorised use.

03 Products & Pricing

We make every effort to display colours, fabrics and details accurately; however, screen settings may cause slight variation and hand-finished pieces naturally differ marginally. Prices are shown in Indian Rupees inclusive of applicable taxes. If a pricing error occurs, we may cancel the affected order and refund you in full.

04 Orders & Acceptance

Placing an order is an offer to purchase. A contract is formed only when we confirm dispatch by email. We may decline or limit an order — for example where an item is out of stock, a price is incorrect, or fraud is suspected — and any amount captured will be refunded in full.

05 Payments

Payments are processed securely by Razorpay via UPI, cards and netbanking. Card details are handled entirely by the gateway and never touch our servers. An order whose payment remains pending may be cancelled automatically. Per-transaction limits imposed by the gateway or your bank may apply.

06 Shipping & Returns

Delivery zones, charges and timelines are set out in our Shipping Policy. Returns, exchanges and refunds are governed by our Return & Refund Policy, which provides a 7-day return window from delivery. Both policies form part of these terms.

07 Coupons & Promotions

  • One coupon per order, unless expressly stated otherwise.
  • Coupons hold no cash value, cannot be exchanged for money, and expire on the stated date.
  • We may withdraw or correct a promotion, and may void discounts obtained through misuse.

08 Reviews

Reviews may be submitted only by customers who purchased and received the item, and must reflect your genuine experience. We do not edit or remove genuine negative reviews. We may remove content that is unlawful, abusive, spam or unrelated. You remain the author and are responsible for what you post.

We are not liable for indirect losses, or for delays caused by events outside our reasonable control. Nothing limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited, including for death, personal injury or fraud.

These terms are governed by the laws of India, with courts of New Delhi having jurisdiction. Grievances are acknowledged within 48 hours and resolved within 30 days as required under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.
