01 Our Promise

Bhuvi Boutique stands behind every piece we ship from our New Delhi studio. Raise a request within 7 days of delivery from My Orders. We review within 24–48 hours, arrange free pickup on approval, and refund to your original payment method after inspection.

02 Return Window

03 Eligibility

  • Unused, unworn, unwashed and unaltered.
  • All original tags, labels and authenticity cards attached.
  • Original Bhuvi Boutique packaging, including dust bags and inserts.
  • Free of perfume, stains, pet hair or smoke odour.
  • Accompanied by the order number from My Orders.
Try-on guidanceTrying a garment on indoors over your own clothing is fine. Items showing outdoor wear cannot be accepted.

04 Non-Returnable Items

  • Innerwear, lingerie, swimwear and pierced jewellery (hygiene).
  • Items marked Final Sale at purchase.
  • Pieces made or altered to your Special Instructions.
  • Gift cards and digital vouchers.
  • Items damaged through misuse after delivery.

05 How to Request

  1. Sign in and open My Orders.
  2. Select 🔄 Return / Exchange on the delivered order.
  3. Choose Return (refund) or Exchange (replacement).
  4. Describe the reason clearly and submit.
  5. We email the decision within 24–48 working hours.

06 Pickup & Inspection

On approval we schedule a free reverse pickup within 2–4 working days. Where pickup is unavailable, ship the item by a trackable service and we reimburse standard costs up to ₹150 against a receipt. Inspection completes within 2 working days of arrival.

08 Exchanges

  • Available for a different size or colourway of the same piece, subject to stock.
  • The requested variant is reserved on approval and dispatched once the original passes inspection.
  • If out of stock, we offer an alternative or convert to a full refund.
  • One free exchange per order line; further changes are treated as a fresh return.

09 Damaged or Wrong Item

Report within 48 hours: photograph the packaging, shipping label and item, and record a short unboxing video. For verified cases we cover all shipping and refund in full including the original delivery fee, or replace free of charge.
